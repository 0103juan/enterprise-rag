# Paid Time Off Policy

## Annual allowance

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued at 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees accrue pro rata. Contractors and interns are not eligible for paid time off.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited. Unused days are not paid out, except on termination where local law requires it.

## Requesting time off

Request leave in Workday. Absences of three or more consecutive days must be requested at least two weeks in advance. Managers must respond within three business days.

## Sick leave

Employees have 10 paid sick days per year, separate from the annual allowance. A doctor's note is required for an absence longer than three consecutive days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
