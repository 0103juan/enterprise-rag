# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and stored in the company password manager. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every account. Access to production systems requires a hardware security key; SMS codes are not accepted for production.

## Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock after five minutes of inactivity. USB mass storage is disabled on all company devices. Personal devices may access email and chat only through the managed mobile profile.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to security@meridianfreight.example within one hour of noticing. The security team remotely wipes the device and rotates the credentials of the owner.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, which includes customer payment details and government identifiers, must never be stored on personal devices or sent to external email addresses. Confidential data may be shared externally only under a signed non-disclosure agreement.
