# On-Call Handbook

## Rotation

Engineers are on call for one week at a time. The handoff happens every Monday at 10:00 local time, with a written summary of open issues. Swaps are allowed if arranged at least 48 hours ahead and recorded in PagerDuty.

## Response times

Acknowledge a SEV1 page within 5 minutes and a SEV2 page within 15 minutes. If the primary engineer does not acknowledge within 10 minutes, the page escalates automatically to the secondary.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a stipend of $400 per week, plus $100 for each page answered outside business hours. An engineer paged between midnight and 06:00 may take a compensatory day off within the following two weeks.
