# Travel and Expense Policy

Effective 1 January 2025. This version replaces the 2023 policy in full.

## Meals

Employees travelling on company business may claim a meal per diem of $75 per day in the United States and €70 per day in the European Union. Alcohol is not reimbursable. The per diem is reduced by half on days where a client or conference provides lunch and dinner.

## Flights

Book economy class for any flight under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a Vice President before booking. All flights must be booked through the Navan travel portal at least 14 days before departure.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel cap is $220. In tier-1 cities (New York, San Francisco and London) the cap is $300 per night. Stays above the cap need manager approval before the trip, not after.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expenses on form FIN-204 within 30 days of the end of the trip. Attach an itemised receipt for every expense over $25. Claims submitted late are paid only with VP approval. Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## Approval limits

A line manager can approve claims up to $2,500. Claims above $2,500 need approval from a Vice President. Nobody may approve their own claim.
