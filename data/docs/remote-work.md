# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Office attendance

Meridian Freight is a hybrid company. Employees attend the office at least two days per week; Tuesday and Thursday are the anchor days for every team. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time.

## Home office stipend

New employees receive a one-time $500 stipend for home office equipment, claimed through the normal expense process. In addition, the company pays $50 per month toward home internet.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year, with approval from HR at least three weeks in advance. Working from a country under trade sanctions is never permitted. Longer stays require a formal relocation and a tax review.
