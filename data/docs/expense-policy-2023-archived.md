# Travel and Expense Policy 2023 (Archived)

ARCHIVED. This policy was superseded on 1 January 2025 and is kept for audit purposes only. Do not use it for current trips.

## Meals (2023, archived)

Under the archived 2023 rules the meal per diem was $60 per day in the United States and €55 per day in the European Union.

## Hotels (2023, archived)

Under the archived 2023 rules the nightly hotel cap was $180 in every city, with no tier-1 exception.

## Submitting a claim (2023, archived)

Under the archived 2023 rules expenses were submitted on form FIN-117 within 45 days of the trip, with receipts required over $50. Form FIN-117 was retired on 1 January 2025.
