# Procurement Policy

## Purchase orders

A purchase order is required for any purchase over $1,000. Invoices without a matching purchase order number are returned to the vendor unpaid. Standard payment terms are net 45 days.

## Competitive quotes

Purchases over $10,000 require three written quotes from independent vendors. A single-source exception must be justified in writing and approved by the Head of Procurement.

## Contract approval

Contracts with a total value over $50,000 must be reviewed by Legal and signed by the Chief Financial Officer. Contracts below that value may be signed by a department head.

## Vendor security review

Any vendor that will store or process Confidential or Restricted data must complete security questionnaire SEC-9 before a contract is signed. The security team has ten business days to review it. Vendors must renew the questionnaire every two years.
