# Data Retention Policy

## Retention periods

Shipment records are kept for 7 years to meet customs requirements. Application logs are kept for 13 months. Database backups are kept for 35 days. Recruitment records for unsuccessful candidates are deleted after 6 months.

## Customer personal data

Personal data of a customer is deleted 90 days after the account is closed. Invoices are exempt and follow the 7-year shipment record period, with personal fields minimised.

## Data subject requests

Requests to access or erase personal data under GDPR must be completed within 30 days. Forward every request to privacy@meridianfreight.example on the day it is received; do not reply to the requester yourself.

## Legal hold

A legal hold issued by the Legal team overrides every retention period in this policy. Data under legal hold must not be deleted, even on request of the data subject, until Legal lifts the hold in writing.
